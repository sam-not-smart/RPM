Abstract 
Hyperfine couplings (hfc's) of the nitrogen nuclei in the 15N-labeled radical cations of chlorophylla and the primary 
donor P7(x) in photosystem I of spinach were investigated in frozen solution by two-dimensional stimulated echo ESEEM. 
JSN hfc tensors were evaluated by comparison of the experimental data with simulations of the time domain an d frequency 
domain ESEEM signals. The results are discussed in the framework of a chlorophylla dimer model for the radical cation 
of PT~N~.

4. Discussion 
4.1. Radical cation [15N]Chla+" 
In this work we have determined four 15N hfc ten- 
sors for the monomeric lsN-Chl a cation radical from 
2D stimulated echo ESEEM experiments. The data ob- 
tained from 15N ENDOR, in which essentially the A± 
elements could be measured [ 16], show good agree- 
ment with those from ESEEM (Table 1). 
From the qualitative evaluation of the 2D HY- 
SCORE spectra in Ref. [16] a lower limit of 4.7 
MHz was estimated for the maximum 15N hfc tensor 
element of [15N]Chla+" which is consistent with a 
broad feature in the 15N ENDOR spectrum assigned 
to a All component [ 16]. The maximum All value in 
the 15N hfc tensor set of [15N]Chla+" obtained from 
the simulations of the ESEEM spectra is 5.50 MHz 
(Table 1 ). Thus, the quantitative results presented in 
this work clearly show that the direct evaluation of 2D 
HYSCORE spectra [ 16] already yielded a satisfying 
estimate of the maximum hfc in this radical cation. It 
is interesting to note that the reliability of this eval- 
uation method is confirmed by inspection of the 2D 
HYSCORE spectrum of [ ]SN] BChl a +" in Ref. [ 22 ]. 
In this case a maximum hfc value of 7.2 MHz was 
concluded from 2D HYSCORE. The highly resolved 
15N ENDOR spectra of [15N]BChla+" [22] yielded 
a value of 7.5 (4-0.1) MHz for the A II component of 
the largest 15N hfc tensor. 
With the exception of our former ENDOR work 
[ 16] all other data of nitrogen hfc's in Chl a +" resulted 
from evaluations of ESEEM experiments [ 27-31 ]. In 
the first investigations nitrogen hfc's were estimated 
from the position of double quantum peaks in 14N 
ESEEM spectra [28,29]. Isotropic 14N hfc's of 2.95
